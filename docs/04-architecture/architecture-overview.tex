\section{Status}
This document records architectural direction during discovery. The final architecture will be based on validated requirements rather than being locked prematurely.

\section{Architectural Intent}
The system should be modular, maintainable, secure, and ready for API-based integration. The MVP should not require every future module to exist, but it should establish clean boundaries around the core clinical workflows.

\section{Cross-Cutting Concerns}
Authentication, authorization, validation, audit logging, error handling, configuration, and notification mechanisms should be treated as platform concerns rather than duplicated inside individual clinical features.

\section{Expansion Strategy}
Future modules such as laboratory, pharmacy, referrals, billing, and patient-facing applications should connect through explicit application/service boundaries. External interoperability will be investigated through standards such as HL7 FHIR where appropriate.

\section{Architecture Decisions}
Important decisions will be recorded separately as Architecture Decision Records (ADRs). Each ADR should explain the problem, the decision, alternatives considered, consequences, and the evidence behind the decision.

\section{What Is Not Decided Yet}
The final frontend/backend technology stack, deployment topology, database implementation details, service granularity, and exact FHIR integration scope remain open until requirements and research provide enough evidence.
