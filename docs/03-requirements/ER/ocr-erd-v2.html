Business rules encoded in FKs

  • Restore = new appointment_history_events row; never delete cancel history.
  • Walk-in = visits.appointment_id NULL.
  • Deceased = patients.status + patient_death_records; app blocks new appointments.
  • No hard-delete of clinical history — soft status / append-only events.

Role write surfaces

  • Reception → patients, appointments, history events, visits
  • Nurse → vital_signs, nursing clinical_notes
  • Clinician → diagnoses, clinical_notes, death records, reports
  • Admin → users, roles, permissions; reads audit_events
